## 🛡️ RIGHTS COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST

**Before sending ANY video to RecCloud, verify:**

- [ ] **License Status:** Is it CC-licensed, public domain, or fair use?
- [ ] **Creator Attribution:** Do you have creator's name and can you credit them?
- [ ] **Explicit Permission:** If not CC-licensed, have you contacted the creator and received written permission?
- [ ] **Educational Use:** Is your use clearly for educational/cultural AI training (non-commercial)?
- [ ] **Derivative Works:** Are you transforming it (transcribing, extracting audio) or using it as-is?
- [ ] **Commercial Intent:** Will Sisi Lola be monetized? If yes, you need stricter licenses.

**Document to keep:**
```
Video Title: [Title]
Creator: [Creator Name]
Source URL: [URL]
License Type: [CC-BY / Fair Use / Permission Email / Public Domain]
Date Acquired: [Date]
Metadata: Creator agreed to use for AI training for non-commercial cultural preservation
```
