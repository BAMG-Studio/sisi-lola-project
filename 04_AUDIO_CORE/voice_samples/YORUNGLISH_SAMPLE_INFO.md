## Phoneme Smoothing Examples

### Problematic Yoruba Phonemes → Pidgin Solution

1. **Ẹ̀ (open-mid front unrounded vowel)**
   - Yoruba: "Ẹ káàbọ̀"
   - Pidgin bridge: "Welcome" → "I dey happy"
   - Result: Smooth transition

2. **Gb (labial-velar plosive)**
   - Yoruba: "Ẹ gbọ́"
   - Pidgin bridge: "You hear?" → "You know wetin?"
   - Result: Natural flow

3. **Tonal complexity**
   - Yoruba: "Àṣà wa" (low-low-mid tones)
   - Pidgin bridge: "our culture" → "e get plenty things"
   - Result: Maintains meaning, easier pronunciation

4. **Nasal vowels**
   - Yoruba: "Ọmọ" (child)
   - Pidgin bridge: "person" → "people"
   - Result: Accessible to non-Yoruba speakers
