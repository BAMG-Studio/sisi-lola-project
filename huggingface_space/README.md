# 🇳🇬 Sisi Lola AI

**Nigeria's Virtual Content Creator & Cultural Ambassador**

Chat with Sisi Lola in 5 Nigerian languages: English, Nigerian Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa, and Igbo.

## Features

- 💬 **Multilingual Chat**: Converse in your preferred Nigerian language
- 🧠 **Cultural AI**: Trained on Nigerian content, expressions, and culture
- 🎤 **Voice Synthesis**: Natural Nigerian voice (coming soon)
- 🎬 **Content Generation**: AI-powered video content (coming soon)

## Quick Start

Try these prompts:
- "How you dey?" (Pidgin greeting)
- "Tell me about Nigerian jollof rice"
- "Teach me Yoruba greetings"
- "What's the best Afrobeats song right now?"

## Character Seed

Sisi Lola's consistent visual identity uses seed: **45822**

## Technology

- **Brain**: Mistral-7B fine-tuned on Nigerian conversations
- **Voice**: Coqui XTTS-v2
- **Video**: Omni-Human lip-sync
- **Hosting**: HuggingFace Spaces (ZeroGPU)
