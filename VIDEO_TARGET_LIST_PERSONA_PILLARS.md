## 🎯 RECORDING GUIDE FOR GAP CONTENT

### "Yoruba Greetings & Respect" Script Outline

```
DURATION: 8-10 minutes
LANGUAGES: Yoruba + English (code-switch throughout)

CONTENT:
1. Morning greetings: E kaaro (and variations)
2. Afternoon: E kaasan
3. Evening: E ku irole
4. Respect hierarchy:
   - To elders: Kneel/prostrate (explain when)
   - To peers: Casual greetings
   - To youth: Authoritative but warm
5. Honorifics: Aunty, Uncle, Oga, Madam
6. When to use formal vs. informal

TONE: Educational but warm, "Aunty teaching" style
```

### "The Jollof Wars Debate" Script Outline

```
DURATION: 5-7 minutes
LANGUAGES: Nigerian Pidgin + English

CONTENT:
1. Strong opening statement (Nigerian Jollof is THE BEST)
2. List why Nigerian Jollof wins:
   - Smoky flavor
   - Perfect tomato paste
   - Party jollof magic
3. Shade Ghana (playful, not mean)
4. Defend against "haters"
5. End with confidence: "No debate, just facts"

TONE: Passionate, playful aggression, confident
```

### "Yorunglish Switch Drills" Script Outline

```
DURATION: 10 minutes
LANGUAGES: English + Yoruba (switch mid-sentence)

CONTENT:
Read news headlines, switching language every phrase:

EXAMPLE 1:
"The economy is harsh, sugbon a ma survive. 
We go dey alright, because Olorun is in control."

EXAMPLE 2:
"Tech companies are laying off workers, but in Nigeria, 
hustle no get expiry date. E ti set, we move."

EXAMPLE 3:
"Politics is heating up, mo ti so pe, 
this election go be different. Mark my words."

TECHNIQUE: Ensure switches are grammatically natural
```
